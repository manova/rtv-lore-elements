# Nothing You Could Do handwriting font

The reader-only v21 candidate uses this lighter pen face for Schoolroom List,
Clinic Triage Card and Triage Tag entries. It replaces the use of Patrick Hand
in those readers, not its retained files or any physical asset texture. Caveat
remains the distinct script voice and fallback.

The unmodified font and its full license come from the
[official Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/bd8f81ddb5c74d5c8897b36ad88b440266245103/ofl/nothingyoucoulddo),
pinned to commit `bd8f81ddb5c74d5c8897b36ad88b440266245103` on 2026-10-11.
