# Torn notebook mesh — curated export record

The selected original Blender mesh was reduced from 9,596 to 4,796 triangles by
paired front/back endpoint collapse. The optimization retained closed geometry,
the torn boundary, front/back UV correspondence and 0.14 mm paper stock.
Nominal dimensions are 135 × 190 mm, with restrained edge curls.

The optimized export passed close, grazing, pickup-distance and underside
authoring review. Its historical GLB SHA256 is
`5a21c5d87174ae208736fbe0d4d64487f6acbfc5f66c5c0202b331788000cf46`.
The GLB and saved Blender checkpoints are not part of this curated source set.

The integrated editable Godot resource is
[torn_notebook_blender_mesh.tres](../../assets/models/torn_notebook_blender_mesh.tres).
The exact current resource hash, atlas, portrait inventory scenes and compatible
legacy paths are recorded in [the package manifest](../../release/0.2.0/package-manifest.json).
Godot integration and gameplay acceptance followed the Blender-only checkpoint;
see [release QA](../../qa/release_0_2_0/README.md).
